QClipz 0.9.0 is the first public pre-release of a fast screen recorder for Windows 10 (2004+) and 11.

**Download:** `QClipz_0.9.0_x64-setup.exe` below. It installs per user with no admin rights.

> The installer isn't code-signed yet. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**. SHA-256: `2b9410c53354b1bb2da567d5f312bb8a28ffbec709cf612d1c5bd781097b79ce`

### Highlights
- Record full screen, a dragged area (with 16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 locks and exact sizes) or a single window.
- System sound with no Stereo Mix needed, plus an optional microphone mix.
- Tear-free Desktop Duplication capture that also works with browsers, video and games.
- QClipz never appears in its own recordings.

### Known limitations
- No pause/resume or global hotkey yet.
- Areas spanning two monitors use slower GDI capture.
- Tested on Windows 10 with two monitors at 100% scaling. Reports from Windows 11, high-DPI setups and dual-GPU laptops are welcome.
